Questions people ask

Do my guests need to install anything?

No. Your link opens in the browser on any phone or computer.

How do guests RSVP?

They tap a few buttons on the page. Their reply reaches you on WhatsApp.

What does the QR code do?

One scan opens your full invite, with events, map, RSVP, games and photos. Print it on your card or share it as an image.

Can I customise it?

Yes. Colours, fonts, photos, music, your story and your games are all made around you.

What do you need from me?

Your names, date, venue and event timings, plus a few photos. Add your story and family details if you want them shown.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what you need, so I quote on WhatsApp. Send your date and what you have in mind and I will reply with a price.
