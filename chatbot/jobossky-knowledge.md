## Who we are

- Jobossky Studios designs and builds digital wedding invites.
- One person, Jobossky, designs and builds every invite. Couples talk to Jobossky directly, from the first message until the day the link goes live.
- Website: https://www.jobosskystudios.online/

## What we make

Two products. The couple picks the size that fits their wedding:

1. **Wedding website**: a full website for the wedding.
2. **Digital invite**: a lighter invite page.

Both come as **one link and one QR code**, with everything guests need inside.

## What an invite can include

Every invite can carry any of these. Couples keep what they like and leave out the rest.

- **Countdown**: days, hours and seconds to the wedding.
- **Events and times**: every function with its time and venue.
- **Map and directions**: one tap opens Google Maps.
- **Photo gallery**: pre-wedding photos to swipe through.
- **RSVP**: guests reply in a few taps, and the reply reaches the couple on WhatsApp.
- **Games and quizzes**: for example "guess the theme" and a couple quiz. Guests can share their score in the family group. Games are built around each couple.
- **Music**: the couple's song, one tap away.
- **Your story**: how the couple met, in their own words.

## Customisation

Everything is made around the couple: colours, fonts, photos, music, story, games and events.

## The QR code

- One scan opens the whole invite: events, map, RSVP, games and photos.
- Couples print it on their invitation card, put it on the welcome board, or send it as an image.
- The QR code's colour matches the invite.

## Why digital invites

- Printed cards get lost. A link stays in every phone.
- PDF invites go unopened. The invite opens instantly in any phone browser, with no download and no app.
- WhatsApp forwards get buried. One link holds everything guests ask about, plus a place to reply.

## How it works (3 steps)

1. **Message on WhatsApp.** Send your names, wedding date, venue and a few photos. That is all that is needed to start.
2. **Get a private preview.** See the invite on your own phone first, then ask for any changes.
3. **Share one link and one QR.** Put them on your invitations, in family groups, or on the welcome board.

No. The link opens in the browser on any phone or computer.
